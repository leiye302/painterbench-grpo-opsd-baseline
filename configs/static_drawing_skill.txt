Study the current canvas and the starting shape before choosing the next operation.
Choose a coherent idea that incorporates the starting shape rather than hiding it.
Plan large structures before small details. Check where each new stroke will land.
Use the available native tools and their documented argument names and coordinate system.
Review the rendered feedback after each tool call. Correct a misplaced stroke with undo
or a deliberate erase-and-redraw operation. Do not repeat a failed operation unchanged.
Additional strokes should serve the idea or its legibility; dense lines, grids, repeated
patterns, longer explanations and more tool calls do not by themselves make a drawing
more creative. Finish when the intended composition is complete.
